# Privacy Policy for AdbApp

Last updated: 2026-09-01

This Privacy Policy explains how AdbApp (package: `get.the.solution.AdbApp`) handles user information when you use the Android application.

## Summary

- AdbApp is designed to execute commands locally on your device.
- We do not require account registration.
- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not use in-app advertising SDKs.

## Information We Collect

AdbApp is designed to minimize data collection.

- We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number, or precise location through the app itself.
- Command input and command output are processed on-device to provide app functionality.
- If you copy content using app features, that content is placed in your device clipboard at your request.

## How Information Is Used

Any information processed by the app is used only to:

- run commands you request,
- display command output in the app,
- let you copy selected output to the clipboard.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

We do not share app data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

We may disclose information only if required by applicable law or valid legal process.

## Data Storage and Retention

- App processing is local to your device.
- We do not maintain user accounts or a cloud database for app user data.
- Data shown in the app may remain on your device (for example, app cache or clipboard) according to your device and Android behavior.

## Permissions

AdbApp declares Android permissions in its manifest to support command execution and related functionality. Depending on Android version, device policy, and OEM restrictions, some permissions may not be granted or usable.

Examples include:

- network access (`INTERNET`),
- storage/system-related permissions,
- package/process/system diagnostic permissions.

Permissions are used only for app functionality and not for advertising profiling.

## Security

We take reasonable technical steps to reduce unnecessary data exposure. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## Children

AdbApp is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Third-Party Services

AdbApp does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

The app is distributed through Google Play, which may process data under its own terms and privacy policies.

## Your Choices

You can:

- stop using the app at any time,
- clear app data and cache in Android settings,
- clear clipboard contents on your device.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by changing the "Last updated" date in this document.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact information listed on the Google Play app listing.
